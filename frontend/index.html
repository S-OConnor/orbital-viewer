Orbital LOS Viewer v0.1.0

Visualizes a satellite in Earth orbit plus up to 5,000 tracked objects in ECEF coordinates. Data flows UDP → C++ Boost backend → WebSocket → plain-WebGL frontend, with no internet access required at runtime.

Press the 3D/SAT toggle (or the V key) to switch to the satellite’s point of view: a 170° fisheye nadir view rendered from the primary satellite’s position, looking straight down at Earth.

Protocol version: 1

UDP protocol · WebSocket protocol

A time-accurate background of stars, the Moon and the naked-eye planets can be enabled in Settings, positioned from the state message’s serverTime.

Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble & Black Marble (public domain).

Note: Sun position is approximate (low-precision almanac formula).
